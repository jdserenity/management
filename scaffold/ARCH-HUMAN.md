## Movement Snacks (Daily Tab)

A "Movement Snack" is a small exercise circuit you do throughout the day. You have a daily goal (default 6, customizable). Each snack has two versions:

- **Hard version** (💪): your ideal — push-ups, squats, reverse crunches
- **Easy version** (🌱): the fallback you do when you don't have it in you — push-ups, reverse lunges, plank

You find movement snacks right in the **Daily tab**, between your stretch routines and your streaks. X / 6 circles fill up as you log snacks.

Every snack you log adds to your day's movement totals. So do morning stretches and exercise breaks from focus flows — they all feed into the same "today's movement" numbers on the Stats page.

### Where everything lives
- **Daily tab**: "Movement Snacks" card with progress bar, hard and easy log buttons
- **Customize tab → Snacks**: edit your daily goal, tweak exercises per version (add, remove, change reps/seconds)
- **Work tab**: the old "Today's movement" card is gone — movement tracking lives in the Daily tab now
